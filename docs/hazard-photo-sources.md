# Hazard photographs: where each one came from

Every photograph on the 18 hazard pages, with its source and licence. Replaced on 6 November 2026, because the previous set had no recorded source or licence (see "Hazard photographs" in `NOTES-INTERNAL.md`).

- **Licence:** all 18 are from Unsplash under the [Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license): free to use, including for commercial and government sites, with no permission or credit required. It does not allow selling unaltered copies or building a competing photo service.
- **Credit:** none is shown on the site, matching the HMGoG decision of 22 October 2026 not to credit stock photos on published posts. The photographer is recorded here instead.
- **Originals:** 2400px downloads, credit files and the licence register row for each are in the brand kit at `Campaigns/Prepare-Gibraltar/2026-11-06-hazard-photos/` and `trackers/stock-licence-register.csv`.
- **Files here:** each hazard has a 1600px `<slug>.webp` for the hazard page and an 800px `<slug>-800.webp` for the index card, both in `src/assets/images/hazards/`.
- **Replacing one:** keep this table current. A photograph with no row here has no recorded licence, which is the problem this table exists to prevent. An image uploaded through the CMS gets no 800px copy; the site falls back to the full-size file.
