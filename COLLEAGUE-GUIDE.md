# Prepare Gibraltar — Content Editor Guide

**For:** Content editors and non-technical staff
**Last updated:** March 2026

---

## What is Prepare Gibraltar?

Prepare Gibraltar is an emergency preparedness website for Gibraltar residents, published by HM Government of Gibraltar — Civil Contingencies Unit. It provides guidance on how to prepare for, respond to, and recover from a range of emergencies.

This guide explains how to update content on the website. You do not need any technical knowledge — everything is done through a web browser, just like updating a document online.

## How to Edit a Hazard Page

Hazard pages are the most commonly updated section. Each one covers a specific type of emergency and has three parts: **Before**, **During**, and **After**.

**Steps:**

1. Click **Hazards** in the left-hand menu
2. Click the hazard you want to edit (e.g. "Flooding")
3. The page will open in the editor
4. Find the section you want to change — scroll down to see **Before**, **During**, and **After**
5. Click into the text and make your changes
6. When finished, click the **Save** button (top right)
7. Then click **Publish** to make your changes live on the website

> **Important:** Clicking Save alone does not update the live website. You must also click **Publish**.

## How to Edit the Homepage

2. Click **Homepage**

4. Click **Save**, then **Publish**

## How to Update Emergency Contact Numbers

1. Click **Emergency Contacts** in the left-hand menu
2. Find the contact you want to update
3. Change the phone number
4. Click **Save**, then **Publish**

> Contact number changes update across the entire website automatically — you do not need to update each page individually.

---

## How to Add a New Hazard Page

1. Click **Hazards** in the left-hand menu
2. Click the **New Hazard** button (top right)
3. Fill in all the fields:
   - **Title** — the name of the hazard (e.g. "Earthquake")
   - **Summary** — one sentence describing the hazard
   - **Who is Most at Risk** — a short paragraph
   - **Before** — what people should do to prepare in advance
   - **During** — what to do while the emergency is happening
   - **After** — what to do once it is over
   - **Local Contacts** — any relevant phone numbers
4. Click **Save**, then **Publish**

The new hazard will automatically appear on the hazards list page — you do not need to add it manually anywhere else.

## Formatting Text

The editor uses simple formatting. You do not need to know any code.

| What you want | How to do it |
|---|---|
| **Bold text** | Select the text, click the **B** button in the toolbar |
| Bullet list | Click the list icon in the toolbar |
| Numbered list | Click the numbered list icon in the toolbar |
| A new paragraph | Press **Enter** twice |
