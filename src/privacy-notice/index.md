This privacy notice explains how HM Government of Gibraltar collects, uses and protects personal data when you use the Emergency Preparedness website.

## Who we are

This website is published by:

- **HM Government of Gibraltar**
- Email: [pressoffice@gibraltar.gov.gi](mailto:pressoffice@gibraltar.gov.gi)

## What data we collect

This website does not collect any personal data. You do not need to register or provide any personal information to use it. We do not use analytics, tracking or any third-party services that collect data about you.

We do not collect your name, email address or any other personally identifying information through this website unless you contact us directly.

## Cookies

See our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/) for full information about the cookies used on this site.

## Your rights

Under the **Gibraltar General Data Protection Regulation (Gibraltar GDPR)** and the **Data Protection Act 2004**, you have rights including the right to access, correct and request erasure of your personal data. For details, contact us using the details above, or contact the <a href="https://www.gra.gi/data-protection" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Gibraltar Regulatory Authority (GRA)<span class="visually-hidden"> (opens in new tab)</span></a>, which acts as the Information Commissioner and supervisory authority for data protection in Gibraltar.

## Changes to this notice

We will update this privacy notice when our data practices change. The date of the last update will be noted at the bottom of this page.

*Last updated: March 2026.*
