This cookie policy explains how the Emergency Preparedness website uses cookies.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit.

## Cookies we use

This website does not use any cookies. We do not collect analytics data, track your browsing, or store any information on your device.

## Third-party cookies

We do not use any third-party services that set cookies on this website.

## Questions

If you have any questions about how we use cookies, contact HM Government of Gibraltar:

- Email: [pressoffice@gibraltar.gov.gi](mailto:pressoffice@gibraltar.gov.gi)
